# Security policy

## Reporting a problem

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

Report it privately on this repository: open **Security**, then **Report a vulnerability**. Include what you found, how to reproduce it and what it affects. You will get a reply within a week.

## In scope

- The site and API at `https://cheesytcg.app`
- The stream overlay and the Twitch Extension panel
- The TCG Bridge Windows app

Examples: signing in as someone else, reading or changing another player's collection or trades, getting staff access, saving pulls you did not make, leaking secrets.

## Out of scope

- Denial of service, load testing or spam against the site
- Attacks on Twitch, Cloudflare, GitHub or other services we use
- Social engineering of staff or players

Never change other players' data while testing, and stop as soon as you have shown the problem.
