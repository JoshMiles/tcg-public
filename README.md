# The Cheesy TCG

**A trading card game played live on stream.**

Viewers on the [Cheesygidds Twitch channel](https://www.twitch.tv/cheesygidds) pull cards on the stream overlay,<br>then collect, trade and show them off on [cheesytcg.app](https://cheesytcg.app).

## Play

The game runs only on the [Cheesygidds Twitch channel](https://www.twitch.tv/cheesygidds). Watch the stream to pull cards, then sign in with Twitch on [cheesytcg.app](https://cheesytcg.app) to see your binder, fill the card dex, climb the leaderboard and trade with other players.

## Help and feedback

- Questions: the [FAQ](https://cheesytcg.app/faq).
- Ideas and problems: the [Feedback page](https://cheesytcg.app/feedback) (sign in with Twitch, pick Suggestion or Problem). We answer in your bell.
- Security problems: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Please don't post them in public.

## TCG Bridge

TCG Bridge is the small Windows app that links the stream PC to the game. It is for the stream team only and signs in with a staff Twitch account.

- Download the latest version: [cheesytcg.app/api/bridge/updates/latest](https://cheesytcg.app/api/bridge/updates/latest)
- It keeps itself up to date.
- The installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you: press **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
